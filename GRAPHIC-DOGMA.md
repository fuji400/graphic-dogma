## Type

1. Display mincho — Noto Serif JP 900, scaleX(0.72–0.86) — title cards only
2. Condensed grotesk — Barlow Condensed — labels, verbs
3. Telemetry mono — IBM Plex Mono, tabular
4. Ceremonial — Cormorant Garamond — SEELE identity only

## Geometry / motion

Radius 0. One hero instrument per view. Motion reveals state.

- Alert: 1s on / 1s off, steps, no fade
- Chevrons: stagger fill, red head on every bar
- MAGI think: 140–220ms flicker, then lock
- Colony: irregular neighbor spread, hold static, 420ms vertical phosphor wipe
- Type flash: hold / cut / empty — no dissolve
- Counters hunt; they never ease to a round number
- AT field: concentric octagons, amber-orange, plot then throb
- Monolith: arm chirp → red letter glow → fifth → shutdown cutoff

## Sound

Synthesized interface tones only. No OST.

- Clock: tick / two-tone under 15s / low stop
- MAGI: think ticks, lock interval
- Colony: scrape, wipe, two pings on clear
- NERV: two-square on ABORT/CUT
- SEELE: arm chirp, speak fifth, off cutoff

## Ten laws

1. One faction per screen
2. One hot accent
3. Radius is zero
4. Motion reveals state
5. Type does the heavy lifting
6. Negative space is tension
7. Repeat the array
8. Color is support (label + shape + position)
9. Geometry before texture
10. Copy the language, not the asset
